# S02-T03 — Policy and audit enforcement

Status: COMPLETE FOR SYNTHETIC IMPLEMENTATION

Implemented `python/budimind_auth/policy.py` as the shared deny-by-default policy primitive used by both APIs:

- Exact plane and role matching is required.
- Tenant mismatch, missing purpose, and missing care/assignment relationship are denied generically.
- Correlation IDs are generated per policy context.
- Audit emission accepts only allow-listed decision fields and writes to the append-only private audit table.
- Actor references are immutable audit identifiers; removing an actor cannot mutate historical audit rows.
